What CINZA does

Point your camera at a paused film, a poster, or a screenshot on another screen. CINZA reads the scene, searches the movie database, verifies the candidates against each other, and shows you the title with an honest confidence score.

It is not a worldwide frame database. Timestamps are only shown when they can actually be established, and when they cannot, the app says so instead of guessing.

Privacy

Uploaded images are processed in memory and never written to disk. Only a small preview thumbnail and non-reversible perceptual hashes are kept, and they are deleted together with the history entry.

Recognition works without an account. Anonymous recognitions stay on this device and cannot be read through the API.
